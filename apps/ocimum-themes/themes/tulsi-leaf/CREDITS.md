# Tulsi Leaf

- **Colours:** TulsiOS. The leaf green (#3f8a4f) is the TulsiOS start button's
  and the dwm bar's; the greys follow Mint-Y-Dark (Linux Mint, GPL-3.0).
- **Background:** Tulsi Leaf (`tulsios-leaf.png`), TulsiOS artwork, shipped in
  the Ocimum Desktop (github.com/lukskrp/ocimum-desktop).
- **Format:** Omarchy's colors.toml (github.com/omacom/omarchy), MIT licence,
  (c) David Heinemeier Hansson.

palettes' extra tones, the terminal colour order and the KDE colour-scheme
layout (`tulsi_theme/kde.py`) are adapted from Omarchy,

## Mint-Y

The GTK 3 and Cinnamon themes are Linux Mint's Mint-Y
(https://github.com/linuxmint/mint-themes, GPL-3.0), recoloured at switch time
the way Mint builds its own colour variants.

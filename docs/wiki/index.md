# TulsiOS Wiki

Help for the things TulsiOS does differently. Every page is on your computer; no
connection needed.

- [AI agents and Starah](ai-agents.html): what coding agents (Claude Code, Codex,
  OpenCode, …) may see and change, how to give them more, and your own rules.
- [Your files' history](file-history.html): getting back deleted or changed files,
  and the trash.
- [Editing files](editing-files.html): Fresh, the terminal editor, its keys, and
  changing system files with `sudoedit`.
- [Drivers](drivers.html): drivers for the graphics card and Wi-Fi (NVIDIA, AMD,
  Intel, Broadcom), and what to do if the screen stays black.
- [Windows programs](windows-programs.html): Bottles for Windows programs, Lutris
  and Steam for games, and why they run as you.
- [Sharing files and networks](sharing.html): LocalSend between your phone and
  computers, Tailscale, and LibreOffice.
- [Printers and scanners](printing-scanning.html): USB and network printers/scanners,
  Document Scanner, and pre-installed drivers.
- [Disk space](disk-space.html): what TulsiOS cleans up on its own, and how to free
  more.

Open these pages from the app menu (**TulsiOS Wiki**), from the Welcome window, or
in a terminal with `tulsi-wiki` (for example `tulsi-wiki ai-agents`).
